01 // PROFILE OVERVIEW

About Me

Samarveer Singh Mertia is a first-year B.Tech Electronics and Communication Engineering student at JECRC University, Jaipur, specializing in Semiconductor and Chip Design.

He is currently developing his foundation in programming, electronics fundamentals, digital logic, semiconductor concepts, and hardware technologies. With a strong interest in understanding how silicon switches billions of times per second to power modern computing, he is focusing his early engineering education on mastering fundamental principles first.

His current programming focus is C programming, which serves as the bedrock for understanding computer architecture, memory manipulation, and embedded hardware-software interactions.
